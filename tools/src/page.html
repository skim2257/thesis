Reproducible Deep Learning for Head and Neck Radiotherapy

Sejin Kim

Deep learning promises individualized prognosis in head and neck cancer, but the data are not analysis-ready, the models are not comprehensively evaluated, and the outputs are rarely aligned with a decision a clinician actually makes.
